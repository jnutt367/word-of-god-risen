# About page wording — DRAFTS (not applied)

Prepared 2026-10-09 for Jason's review. Jason read the current About page
(`app/about/page.tsx`) and said he'd like to work on the wording together —
he feels it's lacking something but isn't sure what. These are options to
react to, not decisions. Current live copy is unchanged.

## What's missing (diagnostic)

1. **The audio Bible is the site's most distinctive feature and isn't named.**
   All 1,503 chapters in Grandpa Teddy's voice — the body copy never says it.
   A seeker reading the About page has no idea the whole Bible can be listened to.
2. **The lost books are the search moat and aren't named in the body.**
   "Enoch, Jasher, Jubilees, and the Apocrypha" appear only in the page
   metadata. Visitors should see the words themselves.
3. **Jason's story is vague ("a man like me").** His 8-years-sober testimony
   anchors the whole channel — the About page is where a newcomer would expect
   to meet the man behind it. One honest line does more than a paragraph of
   abstraction. (Kept light in the drafts — he can dial it up or down.)
4. **The feature paragraph reads like a checklist.** "Read full chapters with
   room to breathe. Follow the cross-references..." — functional, not warm.
5. **No invitation to the YouTube channel in the body copy.** The channel is
   the top of the funnel; the About page should point there warmly, not just
   in the link row at the bottom.

What stays the same in both drafts: John 17:3 opener, the warm
family-oriented tone, the mission ("TruVINE is an extension of our faith in
Jesus"), the not-for-profit honesty, the "one heart finds Jesus" close, the
Cash App support line.

## Draft A — "The fuller story"

> Word of God Risen is a simple Bible reader with a simple conviction:
> God wants us to know Him — "this is eternal life, that they know you,
> the only true God, and Jesus Christ whom you have sent" (John 17:3).
>
> Here you can read the whole Bible — all 66 books, in the WEB and KJV —
> plus the lost books most Bibles leave out: Enoch, Jasher, Jubilees, and
> the Apocrypha. Every chapter can be read or listened to: all 1,503
> chapters are narrated in the warm voice of Grandpa Teddy, who also opens
> each book with a short overview video made by us, never borrowed.
>
> Follow the ✦ cross-references wherever one verse echoes another. Mark
> chapters as read and watch your progress grow. Take your time — there's
> room to breathe here.
>
> TruVINE is an extension of our faith in Jesus — a warm, family home for
> anyone searching for the truth about who He really is. New to faith,
> returning after years away, or just hungry for more: you belong here.
>
> This is a not-for-profit passion project, crafted over years in spare
> hours by a man Jesus pulled out of alcoholism eight years ago — living
> proof that the hopeless can be saved. Every video, every narration,
> every illustration was made with love for the TruVINE family. If one
> heart finds Jesus through this work, that's enough.
>
> You'll find the same heart on our YouTube channel, TruVINE — short
> videos, parables, and encouragement for the road.
>
> If this ministry has blessed you and you'd like to support it, you can
> give via Cash App ($TRUVINE365). Every gift helps keep the Word going out.
