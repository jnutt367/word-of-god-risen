# THE CAST REGISTRY — Character Sheets Master Index

**The mission (Jason, Oct 31, 2026):** "Our mission as disciples is to get to know more about the kingdom of God and our family of ancestors that carried the word throughout history." These sheets are how we know them.

**Jason's ruling (Oct 31, 2026):** this channel is built on these character sheets and on getting their canon right. Every figure who appears in a cover or a film comes from a sheet filed here. Nothing floats.

## Where everything lives
- **Locked portraits (the package):** `public/images/characters/` — crowned faces, the only seeds a build may use (Gate 9).
- **This registry:** `cover-recreation/cast/INDEX.md` — every figure, their file, their status, their books.
- **Per-book side pages:** `cover-recreation/cast/books/<book>.md` — each book's page lists ONLY the characters that book ADDS (figures created for that book). Figures a book merely uses are not repeated there; they live in this index.
- **The road plan:** `cover-recreation/CAST-PLAN-ROAD-AHEAD.md` — who the remaining 60 books need, in production order.
- **Written dossiers (Gospel cast):** `~/workspace/truvine-shorts/mark-full-book/character-sheets/`.

## How a figure graduates
1. Judah builds the portrait, grades it against the package side by side.
2. Jason crowns it. (A heart is not a crown.)
3. On crowning, the portrait is filed into `public/images/characters/`, its status here flips to LOCKED, and it is listed on its book's side page.
4. One-scene faces are cropped from their crowned cover raw and filed the same way (Cast Plan rule 3).

## Status key
- **LOCKED** — crowned, in the package, seedable.
- **AWAITING CROWN** — built and graded, shown to Jason, not yet locked. Never used as a seed until locked.
