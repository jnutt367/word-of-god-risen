# CAST PLAN — THE ROAD AHEAD

**The whole-Bible canon-cover program · cast planned first · written Oct 30, 2026**
**Registry:** the master character-sheet index lives at `cover-recreation/cast/INDEX.md`; per-book addition pages at `cover-recreation/cast/books/` (Jason, Oct 31, 2026).

Covers done: John, Matthew, Mark, Luke, Acts, Romans — **6 of 66**. Remaining: **60 books**.

---

## The rule (Jason, Oct 30, 2026)

1. **Cast is planned first.** No more covers get built until the characters the road needs are planned on paper — this document.
2. **Covers are built only from locked faces.** Every figure who appears on a cover is seeded from a locked package portrait in `public/images/characters/` (Gate 9). No face is ever invented inside a cover build and left floating.
3. **One-scene faces get locked FROM their crowned cover.** When a figure exists for a single book's scene, he or she is created during that cover's build — and the moment Jason crowns the cover, the face is cropped out of the crowned raw and filed into the package, so it can never drift if it is ever needed again.
4. **Jason crowns before anything locks or swaps.** Portraits and covers alike: Judah builds and grades, Jason's eye crowns.

## GROUP C — ONE-SCENE: CREATED AT THE COVER BUILD, LOCKED FROM THE CROWNED COVER

Figures tied to a single book's cover scene. They are built inside that cover's generation, graded like everyone else, and the moment Jason crowns the cover their faces are cropped from the crowned raw into the package (the Tertius rule). Listed for **all 60 remaining books**. Where a book has no clear narrative scene, this plan says so plainly — **scene TBD with Jason** — rather than inventing one.

**Watch-list (one-scene faces that Scripture calls back in other books):** if any of these is created, the lock matters double, because the same face must serve again — **Rahab** (Joshua → named in Hebrews 11 and James 2), **Melchizedek** (a Genesis 14 scene → the heart of Hebrews 5–7), **Nebuchadnezzar** (Daniel → also Jeremiah/Ezekiel's era), **Cyrus** (Ezra → named in Isaiah 45), **Hezekiah** (2 Kings → also Isaiah), **Miriam** and **Caleb** (Exodus/Numbers → Joshua/Judges), **Jonathan** (1 Samuel → 2 Samuel's grief), **Jezebel** (1 Kings → named in Revelation 2). Whichever book builds them first, their locked face governs everywhere after.

A standing reverence note for scene choices: the Father is never depicted as a figure on a cover — voice, light, and glory only (the whirlwind in Job, the visions in Revelation). Jesus, being God made flesh, is depicted from His locked portrait as always.

## PRODUCTION ORDER — Group B portraits, crowned in batches along the road

Portraits are built and crowned **in batches, ordered by the cover road** (the walk runs forward from Romans through the New Testament, then the Old Testament in Bible order when the road turns). Each batch is presented to Jason **as pictures for his crown, one book's needs at a time** — nothing locks uncrowned, and no cover waits on a batch it doesn't need.

1. **Batch 1 — for 1 Corinthians (next):** Timothy · Apollos · Priscilla · Aquila
2. **Batch 2 — for 2 Corinthians & Galatians:** Titus · James the Lord's brother · Barnabas · Silas
3. **Batch 3 — for the prison letters & Philemon:** Epaphras · Luke · Philemon · Onesimus
4. **Batch 4 — for the Pastorals, Peter & Jude:** Jude · (Peter in old age — variant, if Jason rules yes)
5. **Batch 5 — for Revelation:** John in old age (variant, seeded from the locked young John)
6. **Batch 6 — the Pentateuch (when the road turns):** Moses (leader) · Moses (aged) · Aaron · Joshua
7. **Batch 7 — Samuel & Kings:** Samuel · David (youth) · David (king) · Solomon · Elijah · Elisha
8. **Batch 8 — the major prophets:** Isaiah · Jeremiah (young) · Jeremiah (aged) · Ezekiel · Daniel (young) · Daniel (old)
9. **Batch 9 — the post-exile:** Ezra · Nehemiah

Phoebe (flagged optional) joins Batch 3 only if Jason wants her for completeness.

## What this prevents

The Romans cover taught the lesson in one afternoon. Tertius — the scribe in Romans 16:22 — was invented fresh inside the dictation scene because no package portrait existed for him; Jason then had to send the cover back twice (too young, then rendered a miniature beside Paul) before crowning v3, and even after the crown, Tertius's face existed **only inside that one picture** — nowhere in the package — so any future scene needing him would have invented him again, differently, and no one would have caught the drift until Jason's eye did. The same failure had already bitten the parable rebuilds from the other side: generations seeded from earlier generations instead of from the locked files, until the characters on screen were quietly no longer our characters. This plan closes both doors. Recurring faces are crowned once, on paper first, and locked; one-scene faces are locked from the cover the moment it is crowned; and no generation ever seeds from another generation when a locked file exists. What Jason crowns is what the whole Bible will look like — the first time, every time, with nothing to remake.
