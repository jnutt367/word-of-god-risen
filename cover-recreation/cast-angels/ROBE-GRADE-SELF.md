Purpose: test the HEAVENLY robe re-cut (seamless column, fine luminous linen, woven blue bands —
no wrap front, no crossed collar, no sash, no flap, no glowing stitch dashes) against the bathrobe
read of the crowned wrap robe. Exactly 2 generations, no re-rolls. Both plates await Jason's crown.

## GEN 1 — Gabriel (gabriel-ROBE-test-v1.png, seeded from gabriel-CROWNED-lean-regal.png)
- Face identity vs seed: STRONG. Same lean regal man — long fine face, straight nose, grave solemn
  expression, neat swept-back dark hair with faint blue shimmer strands. A touch more stylized/
  smoother than the painterly seed, but unmistakably the same person.
- Bathrobe read: GONE. Seamless column from shoulders to feet, simple round neckline, no wrap,
  no lapels, no sash, no front flap. Blue is flat WOVEN bands — a band at the neckline and a wide
  band at the hem — matte Exodus blue, no glow, no stitch dashes, no embroidery or symbols.
- Eye containment: PASS. Teal-green irises, golden fire held inside the iris boundary, natural
  whites and pupils, skin around the eyes calm. No flame escape, no orb glow.
- Defects: (1) Backdrop departed from the brief — a soft outdoor sunset-hills vista instead of the
  plain warm backdrop (warm-toned, and it rhymes with the seeds' sky, but it is a scene, not a
  plate backdrop). (2) The linen's "bright like lightning" luminosity is modest — the cloth reads
  as clean matte fine linen rather than light-holding radiance.

## GEN 2 — Michael (michael-ROBE-test-v1.png, seeded from michael-CROWNED-mane.png)
- Face identity vs seed: STRONG. Same mature weathered man — heavy furrowed brow, square jaw,
  thick neck, long dark mane past the shoulders with blue shimmer strands. Build reads as briefed:
  tallest frame, broad like a football player's but lean and muscular under the column, never soft.
  Staff planted in the right hand; sword sheathed at his side on the plain brown strap.
- Bathrobe read: GONE. Same seamless column, round neckline with a thin woven blue neck band,
  wide woven blue hem band, no wrap/sash/flap, no glowing piping anywhere.
- Eye containment: NOT MET — the strict standard fails here. The eyes render as luminous
  green-gold GLOWING eyes: the whole iris area is lit, the natural whites are largely lost in the
  glow, and they read as glowing orbs rather than the seed's contained design (golden fire inside
  a teal iris, calm whites and skin). No literal flame tongues lick out and there is no red, but
  the fire is not contained the way the crowned portrait holds it. This is the same failure family
  as the last Michael body plate and is the plate's chief defect.
- Defects: (1) Eye glow as above. (2) Backdrop again an outdoor landscape (grassy hill country)
  instead of the specified plain warm backdrop. (3) Linen luminosity modest, as with Gabriel.

## Verdict of the test
The re-cut answers the bathrobe problem on both men: the seamless column + woven blue bands read
as a heavenly alb, not a bathrobe, and both faces carried. Gabriel's plate is clean apart from the
backdrop departure. Michael's plate still needs its eyes solved — the generator keeps lighting his
eyes into orbs at full-body distance even with the law stated strictly.

## v2 draped (ROBE2 test plates, Oct 31, 2026 — seeded from the CROWNED portraits, style from ROBE-REFERENCE-jason-2026-10-31.png, exactly 2 generations, no re-rolls)

### GEN 1 — Gabriel (gabriel-ROBE2-test-v1.png, seed gabriel-CROWNED-lean-regal.png)
- Face identity vs seed: STRONG. Same lean, regal, grave man, same age; neat dark hair with the blue
  shimmer strands; swift-athlete frame reads under the drape.
- Drape/fold quality vs the reference: MET. A true draped mantle — deep, soft, weighty folds falling
  diagonally across the body over the column under-robe, the reference's cloth behavior carried. No
  sash, no tie, no belt, no wrap collar, no lapels; no embroidery, patterns, or symbols.
- Blue-light-in-cloth read: RIGHT COLOR, HARD EDGE. The blue is electric and never gold, and there
  are no flat woven bands — but the light runs as bright NEON LINES along the seams and the drape's
  edges, with dotted LED-like dashes in places; it reads as stitched light-cord/piping more than
  light held softly inside the fabric. The cloth between the lines does glow softly blue, so the
  direction is right — the edge lines overshoot the reference's softness.
- Eye containment (crop-checked): PASS. Teal irises, visible pupils, natural whites, golden fire
  burning inside both irises; one small flame lick at the top of his right iris spills minimally —
  inside the crowned design's "very minimal overspray." No orb glow, no light cast on the face.
- Backdrop: plain warm, as briefed (the v1 drift is fixed on this plate).
- Defect: the hem pools forward into a train spreading on the ground past his sandals — grounded,
  but more bridal train than a robe's weighted pool.

### GEN 2 — Michael (michael-ROBE2-test-v1.png, seed michael-CROWNED-mane.png, composed LARGE in frame)
- Face identity vs seed: STRONG. Same mature, weathered, heavy-browed man; long mane with blue
  shimmer; tallest knight's build, broad and lean; staff planted in the right hand, sword sheathed.
- Drape/fold quality vs the reference: STRONG drape — deep diagonal folds with real weight over the
  column. The mantle's fall is the best of the four robe plates.
- Blue-light-in-cloth read: OVERSHOT INTO LIGHTNING. Alongside the seam lines, actual electric
  ARC/bolt patterns crackle across the cloth — dramatic lightning figures rather than soft light
  held in linen. Blue and never gold, no flat bands — but it reads as a lightning pattern ON the
  cloth, not radiance IN it.
- Eye containment (crop-checked): FAILED — the worst escape yet. Real flame tongues rise OUT of
  both eyes up to the brow line; the irises themselves burn yellow-green and lit. Composing him
  large in the frame did NOT hold the law. This is Michael's fourth full-body eye failure: the
  generator escapes his fire no matter the stated strictness or the face's scale in frame.
- Defects: (1) Eyes as above — the plate's chief defect. (2) A plain brown WAIST BELT appeared
  holding the scabbard — the brief forbade any belt (sword was to hang on a side strap). (3)
  Backdrop drifted outdoors again (desert hills under a sunset sky) instead of plain warm.

### Verdict of the v2 test
The drape is the answer to the reference — both men wear the mantle's deep diagonal folds, and the
bathrobe is nowhere in either plate. Two open problems: (1) the blue light's rendering — neon piping
on Gabriel, lightning bolts on Michael; the reference's SOFT in-cloth radiance sits between those
two overshoots. (2) Michael's eyes remain unsolved by generation alone — four failures across two
robe rounds and both body plates; his eye answer will have to come from somewhere other than a
stricter brief (post-paint from the crowned portrait is the standing candidate; not attempted here).
