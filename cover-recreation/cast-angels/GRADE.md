# GRADE — THE NAMED ANGELS: Gabriel · Michael (design anchors)

**Built Oct 31, 2026 · exactly 2 generations spent (one per figure, no re-rolls)**
Graded side-by-side against the locked package (`public/images/characters/`): render family, distinctness, Scripture fidelity. Side-by-side sheet: `contact-sheet.jpg` in this folder.
**Status: AWAITING CROWN. Nothing has been copied into `public/images/characters/`.**

Why these exist (Jason's ruling, Oct 31, 2026): scenes will show angels WITHOUT their faces — turned, from behind, or in light — leaning toward Michael or Gabriel in the places each might have stood, never asserting it; it could be any angel. These two portraits are the anchors that keep the leaning figures the same two designs (build, hair, raiment) in every scene. The faceless rule is for scenes; the portraits show their faces so the designs can be locked.

Deliberate fidelity call, both figures: **no wings.** Daniel calls Gabriel "the man Gabriel" (Dan 9:21) and Scripture never describes either angel with wings (wings belong to the cherubim and seraphim of other passages). If Jason wants wings, that is his crown call to add.

## Gabriel — `gabriel.png` (1600×1600) — PASS (three watches)

- Family: match — Pixar-type render, warm golden light, woven cloth, expressive eyes, head-and-shoulders. The backdrop is a luminous dawn sky instead of the package's earthly grove or village: a deliberate difference, fitting for one who stands in God's presence, and still inside the house's warm palette.
- Scripture: a grave grown man in unadorned pure white, no weapons, no armor — the herald who is "sent to speak" (Luke 1:19). Clean-shaven with dark hair swept back to the jaw: "the man Gabriel" (Dan 9:21).
- Distinctness: plainly his own man — finer-featured and lighter-built than Michael beside him on the sheet, and nowhere near Jesus: no center-parted hair past the shoulders, no beard, no freckles, no olive-tan wrap; the robe is pure white only. He does NOT read as a woman: strong jaw, heavy brow, mature masculine features.
- ⚠️ WATCH 1: the expression is grave to the edge of somber — the calm authority landed; the gladness of "good tidings" is quiet in him. Jason's eye rules whether the herald should carry more warmth.
- ⚠️ WATCH 2: his skin is the fairest in the set and his swept hair carries lighter brown tones with a slightly groomed, modern wave — radiance or drift, Jason decides.
- ⚠️ WATCH 3: the crossed collar of the white robe reads faintly like a bathrobe or martial-arts wrap at first glance; in frame it still reads as crossed ancient raiment.

## Michael — `michael.png` (1120×2240) — PASS (three watches)

- Family: match — same render family and warm dawn sky; ivory woven raiment, and the heavier commander's mantle over one shoulder is cloth alone: no armor plates, no clasp, no sword, nothing Roman. He does NOT read as a Roman soldier.
- Scripture: the broadest, strongest build in the package — wide shoulders, thick neck, square jaw — a guardian-commander's bearing. His might is in his stance, per the brief; and the closed mouth and stillness carry Jude 9's restraint: he contends, and leaves the rebuke to the Lord.
- Distinctness: clearly a different man from Gabriel at a glance — heavier face, thicker and wavier hair with more volume, sterner set — and nowhere near Jesus (no long center-parted hair, no beard, no olive-tan wrap).
- ⚠️ WATCH 1: the brow is heavy and lowered; he reads resolute to the edge of scowling. Jason's eye rules whether it stands as guardian resolve or tips toward menace.
- ⚠️ WATCH 2: the massive jaw and build are the most "superhero" silhouette in the package — inside Pixar-type proportions, but the strongest stylization of the set.
- ⚠️ WATCH 3: he came out portrait-ratio (1120×2240) where Gabriel and the package portraits are square. His face sits centered, so a clean square crop exists inside the frame if the package wants squares on graduation.
